# Track 2 narration

## Slide 1

I’m Prasad Khake. My background is software, and I entered this challenge to see what I could contribute to a real biological problem. I used Codex to help investigate the evidence and build analysis tools. My proposal is to test whether nicotinic acid can improve chromosome division in BUBR1-related MVA. Here is what I found, and the experiment those findings justify.

## Slide 2

Nicotinic acid can feed NAD production through NAPRT. Published work connects NAD-dependent SIRT2 activity with BUBR1 stability. Separately, genetic BUBR1 restoration supports testing chromosome-division function. These findings suggest a possibility: preserve useful residual BUBR1. The studies used different interventions and models, so the complete nicotinic-acid-to-functional-rescue chain still needs testing.

## Slide 3

I reanalysed public muscle RNA data from a niacin study in mitochondrial myopathy. Among four patients with paired samples at month four, SIRT2 RNA increased in all four. The magnitude was fragile in sensitivity checks, and month-ten direction was not robust. BUB1B RNA was too sparse to assess. This exploratory result adds context; it cannot establish BUBR1 protein activity or a drug response in MVA.

## Slide 4

The next step is a controlled cell experiment. Establish the defect and a wild-type restoration control, then check NAD engagement and BUBR1 response. The decisive readout is fewer chromosome-segregation errors while cells keep dividing and producing healthy cells. More protein alone is insufficient. Independent replicates and viability measurements would help reject apparent improvement caused by cell death or arrest.

## Slide 5

If that test succeeds, it could justify further work on preserving residual BUBR1 function in suitable MVA contexts. An existing prescription product provides a repurposing anchor. Effective exposure and pediatric safety still need investigation. Increased survival could also protect abnormal cells, so chromosome fidelity, tissue growth and tumor risk need separate evaluation before any clinical consideration.

## Slide 6

My contribution is an inspectable case for testing this candidate, including the evidence against it. I built reproducible public-data analyses and defined outcomes that would support or reject the hypothesis. Others can adapt that workflow, although broader disease validation remains ahead. I’m proposing a focused experiment that could turn a plausible mechanism into a useful answer.
