## Adversarial questions

- **Does a perfect Track 1 score validate this drug?** No. It supplies a candidate
  disease-gene context, not drug-response evidence.
- **Is NAD deficiency known in this child?** No. Measure it; do not assume it.
- **Would more defective protein help?** Unknown. Require useful residual function
  and segregation measurements, not abundance alone.
- **Could fewer abnormal cells reflect killing or arrest?** Yes. Track cell fate,
  completed divisions and absolute output alongside error rates.
- **Does the mouse lifespan experiment establish NMN or niacin efficacy?** No.
  Its intervention was genetic SIRT2 overexpression; phenotype rescue was partial.
- **Could benefit be mediated by other SIRT2 targets?** Yes. The source authors
  acknowledge this; add BUBR1-specific causal comparisons.
- **Are all sources fully audited?** No. North's supplied supplements are reviewed;
  other sources have the bounded depths above. No raw-data reanalysis was done.
- **Is this the best of all approved drugs?** Not established. It is a conditional,
  testable lead selected from the documented comparison, not an exhaustive ranking.

## Package review disposition

No central claim requires a new efficacy assertion. Keep all proposed experiments
in future/conditional tense. Preserve the null and conflicting studies, unknown
historical AI settings/costs and absence of laboratory commitments. Private emails
are neither quoted nor used as endorsement. The final participant review and
external release decision remain separate from this local audit.
